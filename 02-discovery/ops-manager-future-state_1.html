Future-state journey · Simplification initiative

From parallel systems to one trusted record

Persona
Regional / enterprise ops manager who bought for admin and reporting, and must justify renewal.
Goal
One reliable record their team actually uses, so reports reflect what really happened.
Strategy
Reclaim operational simplicity: strip legacy noise, make everyday work the fastest path.
Why launch now

One renewal is flagged at risk. One enterprise account is evaluating a routing-only competitor (UXR-04, UXR-10). Without simplification, our market position becomes untenable.

3 advantages over the manual workaround

One record, not three

Captures each delivery once → one auditable record replaces WhatsApp, texts and paper

Reports that build themselves

Feeds reports from live app data → no manual reconciliation before every review

Scales without extra staff

Onboards drivers same day → fleet grows without shadowing or parallel coordination

Dependency: Stage 3 also needs the sync delays (BUG-2044, BUG-2072) and mid-route crash (BUG-2031) fixed. Simplicity alone won't retire WhatsApp.
