⚡ Strategic tensions to debate

T1 · Nudge enough vs. become noise

The user's core job is “remind me so I don't have to remember,” but notification fatigue is the fastest route to a muted, then deleted, app.

Debate: Do we optimise for reminders sent or reminders acted on? Fewer, better-timed nudges may lower short-term logging but protect long-term retention.

T2 · What sits behind the paywall?

Smart, context-aware reminders are both the most valuable feature and the strongest premium hook.

Debate: Paywall them and free users never reach the “aha” moment. Give them away and premium has to stand on insights alone, which may be too weak to convert.

T3 · B2C app vs. B2B wellness product

Corporate wellness offers bigger, stickier contracts, and our 25 to 40 working adults are exactly who employers want to reach.

Debate: The buyer (HR) wants aggregate reporting and engagement stats. The user wants privacy and simplicity. Chasing both in 12 weeks risks building two half-products.

T4 · The success-churn paradox

If the app works, users form the habit and may no longer need it. Our best outcome for the user looks like churn on the dashboard.

Debate: Do we accept graceful churn and price for it (one-time or short programmes), or expand the value (energy, focus, wider health) to earn a long-term place on the phone?

T5 · Streaks motivate and punish

Streaks drive early engagement, but one missed day can make an all-or-nothing user quit entirely.

Debate: Hard streaks for stronger short-term pull, or forgiving progress (weekly targets, streak freezes) for steadier long-term retention?
